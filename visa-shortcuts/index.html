    Visa Shortcuts — easier entry with the visas you already hold

    Many countries cut the visa-application red tape if you already hold a visa or residence permit for a major economy. Holders of a valid US, UK, Ireland, Canadian or Schengen visa can often get an eVisa, visa-on-arrival, or even visa-free entry to countries that would otherwise demand a full embassy application. Browse every shortcut we've documented, by your passport.

    Free · Sources linked per row · Last reviewed 24 May 2026

    How to use a shortcut. Carry the qualifying visa physically (or a printed copy) along with the passport that contains it. At eVisa application or border control you'll be asked to upload / show it. Most online portals walk you through proof submission.

    The qualifying visa must be valid on the day of entry. A US visa that expires next week won't unlock a Türkiye eVisa for a trip in 3 months.

    Diplomatic / service / special passports have their own set of shortcuts — usually more generous. Holders should check the destination's MFA page for bilateral agreements specific to those passport types.

    Have a shortcut we should add? Open an issue at github.com/Uygara/atlas-visa-globe.
