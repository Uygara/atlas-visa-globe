Visa answers on your agency's website

Your customers ask whether they need a visa before they book. Put a visa checker on your own site, under your logo, that answers for 200 passports and every destination and is updated every day.

This is a pilot. We are looking for the first travel agencies, tour operators and visa consultants to use it. Pilot agencies pay nothing until the version under their own brand is live on their site. Tell us below what you would pay; we reply to every request within two working days.

What the checker looks like

This is the free version that any site can embed today. The agency version is the same box with your logo and colours instead of ours.

What the agency version includes

  • Your brand, not ours. Your logo and colours, no travelnow.info link, on as many pages of your site as you like.
  • A source on every answer. Each answer shows where the rule comes from and when it was last checked. For the Schengen area, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Thailand and Türkiye the rules are checked against the destination's official list.
  • Change emails. An email the day a rule changes for the destinations you sell.
  • Fast corrections. Report a wrong answer and we check it against the official source within one working day.

The checker gives information, not legal advice. The final decision on entry is always the border authority's, and each answer links to the official source where we have one.

  • Data controller: Uygar Atalay, travelnow.info, hello@travelnow.info.

  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a); KVKK articles 5 and 9).
  • Where it is stored: in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), on Google servers in the European Union. Nobody can read the requests from the website.
  • How long: for as long as we talk about the pilot, and at most 12 months unless you become a customer.
  • Your rights: you can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to hello@travelnow.info from the address you used.
