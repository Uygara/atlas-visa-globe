Smart travel planner

Plan a trip with one or more countries. Add your stops and dates, and the planner shows what each country asks of your passport: visa-free entry, an eVisa, a visa on arrival, a visa from an embassy or an online travel authorisation, with the allowed stay, the data date and the source.

Schengen stops are counted together against the 90/180-day rule. You also get airport transit rules, government safety advice, the passport validity rule for each country and a checklist you can tick off. It works without an account; sign in to keep your trips on every device.
