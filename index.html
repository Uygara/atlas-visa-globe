How the data is built

Visa requirements are re-scraped daily from public visa-policy pages and authoritative government sources, on top of a hand-curated correction layer that is checked against official sources. Transit-visa lists come from the UK's Immigration (Passenger Transit Visa) Order and the EU Visa Code. We never invent data: if a fact can't be sourced, we leave it out. Always verify with the destination's embassy before booking — see our About, Terms and Privacy pages.
