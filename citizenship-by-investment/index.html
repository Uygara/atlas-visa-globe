    Citizenship by Investment — 2026

    Every active citizenship-by-investment (CBI) and major residence-by-investment (RBI / Golden Visa) programme on a single page. Minimum investment, qualifying routes, realistic timeline, visa-free score of the resulting passport, and whether your spouse and children can be added.

    CBI vs RBI. CBI grants a second passport directly, usually after a 4-12 month process. RBI (Golden Visa) grants a renewable residence permit; you can apply for citizenship later through the country's regular naturalisation timeline (typically 5-10 years of physical residency, depending on the jurisdiction).

    Minimum investment on this page is the cheapest qualifying route, before government fees, due-diligence fees, legal fees, and (where applicable) real-estate transaction costs. Realistic all-in for a Caribbean family-of-4 CBI is $150K-220K including all fees; Malta/Türkiye is closer to $700K-$1M.

    Visa-free score is the Henley-Passport-Index style count of destinations accessible without a visa (or with eVisa / visa-on-arrival) for the resulting passport. For RBI rows we show "—" because residence permits don't change your existing passport's visa-free access.

    Tax planning. A passport changes your travel options. Your tax residence is a separate question — most countries on this list tax residents on worldwide income, and a few apply territorial regimes. Always work with an international tax advisor before treating any CBI/RBI as a tax-optimisation strategy.
