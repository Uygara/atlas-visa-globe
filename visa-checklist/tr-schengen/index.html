Schengen Tourist Visa — Turkish Citizens

Last reviewed 2026-05-24 · Free · Sources linked below

Your situation

Which consulate to apply to? Apply to the consulate of the Schengen state where you'll spend the most days. If days are equal, apply to the one you'll enter first. Some countries (Germany, France) have higher volume but faster processing — others (Italy, Spain) can have multi-week wait times even for the appointment.

The cascade rule means clean applicants typically get 1-year, 2-year, then 5-year multi-entry visas. See the Schengen calculator page for the cascade tier you should request.
