Passport power changes, November 2026

This month is not over. The report covers changes recorded up to 21 November 2026.

So far in November 2026, our daily snapshot recorded 20 visa-policy changes affecting 15 passports and 14 destinations. Counted as destinations that need no visa arranged in advance (visa-free, ID card travel, travel authorization and visa on arrival), 4 passports gained on balance and 6 lost.

Biggest gains

Passports ranked by the net change in destinations with easy access (visa-free, ID card travel, travel authorization or visa on arrival).

  • +2 🇹🇼 Taiwan
  • +2 🇹🇱 Timor-Leste
  • +1 🇮🇶 Iraq
  • +1 🇰🇼 Kuwait

Biggest losses

  • -1 🇧🇾 Belarus
  • -1 🇲🇪 Montenegro
  • -1 🇷🇺 Russia
  • -1 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia
  • -1 🇹🇹 Trinidad and Tobago
  • -1 🇹🇷 Turkey

How to read this

These are changes recorded in our daily snapshot of public visa-policy data (Wikipedia's visa requirement articles). A change can reflect a correction in the source rather than a new rule. Check the destination's official site before you travel. Within a month, a change that was later reversed is left out, so only the net result is counted.
