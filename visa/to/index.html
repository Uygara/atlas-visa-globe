    Tonga entry: frequently asked questions

    Do citizens of the United States need a visa for Tonga?

    Not in advance. Citizens of the United States can get a visa on arrival in Tonga (up to 31 days).

    Do citizens of the United Kingdom need a visa for Tonga?

    Not in advance. Citizens of the United Kingdom can get a visa on arrival in Tonga (up to 31 days).

    Do citizens of India need a visa for Tonga?

    Yes. Citizens of India need a visa for Tonga arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate.

    Do citizens of China need a visa for Tonga?

    No. Citizens of China can enter Tonga visa-free for up to 30 days.

    Do citizens of Germany need a visa for Tonga?

    No. Citizens of Germany can enter Tonga visa-free for up to 90 days.

    Do citizens of Canada need a visa for Tonga?

    Not in advance. Citizens of Canada can get a visa on arrival in Tonga (up to 30 days).

    Do citizens of Australia need a visa for Tonga?

    Not in advance. Citizens of Australia can get a visa on arrival in Tonga (up to 31 days).

    Do citizens of Turkey need a visa for Tonga?

    Not in advance. Citizens of Turkey can get a visa on arrival in Tonga (up to 31 days).
