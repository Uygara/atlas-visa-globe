Before you go

  • Passport validity: the minimum on record for Hong Kong is 1 months beyond your intended stay. Check your own passport date.
  • Travel advisories: UK FCDO: normal precautions; US State Department: increased caution; Government of Canada: increased caution. Levels as of 21 November 2026. Open the safety map.
  • Airport transit: nationalities that need a transit visa when changing planes in Hong Kong: Afghanistan, Albania, Cuba, Laos, Mongolia, Nigeria, North Korea, Somalia, Sri Lanka, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, and Vietnam. Exemptions apply. Open the transit map.

Hong Kong entry: frequently asked questions

Do citizens of the United States need a visa for Hong Kong?

No. Citizens of the United States can enter Hong Kong visa-free for up to 90 days.

Do citizens of the United Kingdom need a visa for Hong Kong?

No. Citizens of the United Kingdom can enter Hong Kong visa-free for up to 90 days.

Do citizens of India need a visa for Hong Kong?

Yes, an eVisa. Citizens of India apply online before travelling to Hong Kong.

Do citizens of China need a visa for Hong Kong?

Yes. Citizens of China need a visa for Hong Kong arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate.

Do citizens of Germany need a visa for Hong Kong?

No. Citizens of Germany can enter Hong Kong visa-free for up to 90 days.
