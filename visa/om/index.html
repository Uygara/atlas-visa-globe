    Oman entry: frequently asked questions

    Do citizens of the United States need a visa for Oman?

    Yes, an eVisa. Citizens of the United States apply online before travelling to Oman.

    Do citizens of the United Kingdom need a visa for Oman?

    Yes, an eVisa. Citizens of the United Kingdom apply online before travelling to Oman.

    Do citizens of India need a visa for Oman?

    Yes. Citizens of India need a visa for Oman arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate.

    Do citizens of China need a visa for Oman?

    Yes, an eVisa. Citizens of China apply online before travelling to Oman.

    Do citizens of Germany need a visa for Oman?

    Yes, an eVisa. Citizens of Germany apply online before travelling to Oman.

    Do citizens of Canada need a visa for Oman?

    Yes, an eVisa. Citizens of Canada apply online before travelling to Oman.

    Do citizens of Australia need a visa for Oman?

    Yes, an eVisa. Citizens of Australia apply online before travelling to Oman.

    Do citizens of Turkey need a visa for Oman?

    No. Citizens of Turkey can enter Oman visa-free for up to 30 days.
