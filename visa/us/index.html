Before you go

  • Passport validity: for the United States the rule on record is that the passport must be valid for the length of your stay. Check your own passport date.
  • Travel advisories: Government of Canada: normal precautions. Levels as of 22 November 2026. Open the safety map.
  • ESTA: travellers entering under the Visa Waiver Program need an approved ESTA (fee $40.62 USD, valid 2 years). ESTA rules.
  • Airport transit: nationalities that need a transit visa when changing planes in the United States: all nationalities. Exemptions apply. Open the transit map.

The United States entry: frequently asked questions

Do citizens of the United Kingdom need a visa for the United States?

Not a visa, but citizens of the United Kingdom need an online travel authorization for the United States before departure.

Do citizens of India need a visa for the United States?

Yes. Citizens of India need a visa for the United States arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate.

Do citizens of China need a visa for the United States?

Yes. Citizens of China need a visa for the United States arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate.

Do citizens of Germany need a visa for the United States?

Not a visa, but citizens of Germany need an online travel authorization for the United States before departure.

Do citizens of Canada need a visa for the United States?

No. Citizens of Canada can enter the United States visa-free for up to 180 days.

Do citizens of Australia need a visa for the United States?

Not a visa, but citizens of Australia need an online travel authorization for the United States before departure.

Do citizens of Turkey need a visa for the United States?

Yes. Citizens of Turkey need a visa for the United States arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate.
