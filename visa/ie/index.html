Before you go

  • Passport validity: for Ireland the rule on record is that the passport must be valid for the length of your stay. Check your own passport date.
  • Travel advisories: UK FCDO: normal precautions; US State Department: normal precautions; Government of Canada: normal precautions. Levels as of 26 November 2026. Open the safety map.
  • Airport transit: nationalities that need an airport transit visa when changing planes in Ireland, even without leaving the transit area: Afghanistan, Albania, Bolivia, Botswana, Cuba, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Dominica, Eritrea, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Georgia, Ghana, Honduras, Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Lesotho, Moldova, Nauru, Nicaragua, Nigeria, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint Lucia, Somalia, South Africa, Sri Lanka, Trinidad and Tobago, Vanuatu, and Zimbabwe. Exemptions apply. Open the transit map.

Ireland entry: frequently asked questions

Do citizens of the United States need a visa for Ireland?

No. Citizens of the United States can enter Ireland visa-free for up to 90 days.

Do citizens of the United Kingdom need a visa for Ireland?

No. Citizens of the United Kingdom can enter Ireland visa-free.

Do citizens of India need a visa for Ireland?

Yes. Citizens of India need a visa for Ireland arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate.

Do citizens of China need a visa for Ireland?

Yes. Citizens of China need a visa for Ireland arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate.

Do citizens of Germany need a visa for Ireland?

No visa is needed. Citizens of Germany can enter Ireland with a national ID card.

Do citizens of Canada need a visa for Ireland?

No. Citizens of Canada can enter Ireland visa-free for up to 90 days.

Do citizens of Australia need a visa for Ireland?

No. Citizens of Australia can enter Ireland visa-free for up to 90 days.

Do citizens of Turkey need a visa for Ireland?

Yes. Citizens of Turkey need a visa for Ireland arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate.
