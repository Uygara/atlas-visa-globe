Since 10 April 2026, the EU Entry/Exit System (EES) records every entry and exit of non-EU nationals on a short stay at the Schengen external border, with fingerprints and a facial image. It applies whether or not you need a visa, and it has replaced passport stamps.

Worth knowing: days spent on an EU residence permit or a long-stay (D) visa do not count towards the 90/180 short-stay limit.
