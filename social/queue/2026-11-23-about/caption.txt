travelnow.info is a free visa map for every passport. Pick yours and see, country by country, where you can go visa-free, with a visa on arrival, with an eVisa, or only with a visa arranged in advance.

Also on the site: transit rules, a Schengen 90/180 calculator, passport validity checks and official travel advice. Data refreshed every day. No sign-up.

What we post here: visa rule changes, passport rankings and short guides to the rules that catch travellers out.
