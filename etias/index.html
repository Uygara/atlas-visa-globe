    ETIAS — Europe's new travel authorization

    From late 2026, every non-EU visitor entering the Schengen Area without a visa will need an ETIAS (European Travel Information & Authorization System) approval. US, UK, Canadian, Australian, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian and 60+ other passport holders are affected. €7, valid 3 years, applied online in minutes.

    Last reviewed 2026-05-24 · Source: EU Commission

    Who's affected

    If your country's passport currently lets you enter Schengen visa-free for short stays (and you're not already an EU / EEA / Swiss citizen), ETIAS will apply to you. The full list of 60+ affected nationalities includes: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Mexico, Israel, Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan, UAE, Albania, Serbia, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Bosnia & Herzegovina, and most Caribbean and South-Pacific states. Use the checker above for your specific passport.

    What's NOT covered by ETIAS

    • EU / EEA / Swiss citizens — freedom of movement applies; nothing changes.
    • Travellers who already need a Schengen visa (Turkey, India, China, Russia, most African and Middle-Eastern passports) — keep applying for visas as usual.
    • Long-stay (more than 90 days) — that's a national visa or residence permit, not ETIAS.
    • Work / study / family reunification — full visa categories, not touched by ETIAS.
    • Ireland — not part of Schengen; no ETIAS needed for Ireland.

    How to apply (when launched)

    1. Open the official ETIAS portal: travel-europe.europa.eu/etias_en. Beware of scam sites — only use the .europa.eu domain.
    2. Fill in passport details, travel plans, criminal history questions.
    3. Pay the €7 fee with a credit/debit card.
    4. Most applicants get approval within 96 hours — keep your ETIAS number handy at the airport.
    5. Authorization is tied to your passport. Renew when the passport expires.

    Common questions

    Is ETIAS a visa? No. It's a pre-travel security authorization, similar to the US ESTA. You still go through normal border control on arrival.

    What if I'm refused? You'll get a reason (criminal record, prior overstay, security concern). You can appeal, or apply for a regular Schengen short-stay visa instead.

    What if I have dual citizenship? Apply with the passport you'll travel on. If one of your passports is EU/EEA, use that — no ETIAS needed.

    What happens at the border? The carrier checks ETIAS before boarding. Schengen border guards check on arrival. Without it, you can be refused boarding.
