    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 24 September 2026 · Operator: travelnow.info (Uygar Atalay)

    What this site is

    travelnow.info (travelnow.info) is a free reference tool that shows visa requirements for travelers worldwide. The dataset is rebuilt every 24 hours from authoritative public sources. An account is optional: everything works without one, and we only ask for an email address if you choose to create one.

    Information we collect ourselves

    Aggregate, anonymous usage statistics only — via Google Analytics 4 (with IP anonymisation enabled) and Matomo Tag Manager. These help us see which pages are read and on which devices, so we know what to improve. Neither receives an identifier we hold; both set their own cookies (see "Cookies" below).

    For first-time visitors, the browser's time zone is used locally to guess a likely passport. It never leaves your browser, and we never ask for your location.

    If you subscribe to alerts

    The travelnow.info alerts service stores only what's needed to deliver mail: your email address, the country codes you chose to watch, your tier (free or Pro), and timestamps. Pro subscribers' Stripe customer + subscription IDs are also stored so we can manage billing — Stripe holds the actual card data, never travelnow.info. Records live in Cloudflare KV in the EU region.

    Every alert email contains a one-click unsubscribe link that deletes your record immediately and (if applicable) cancels your Stripe subscription.

    If you create an account

    Accounts are optional. You can sign in with Google, with an email address and password, with a link sent to your email, or with your phone number (we send you a one-time code by SMS). If you sign in, we store your email address or phone number and the things you save on the site so they are available on your other devices: your passport and passport type, residence permits you added, watched destinations, your planned trip, Schengen calculator trips, and your language and theme. We never ask for passport numbers or documents.

    Sign-in and storage are provided by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, hosted in the EU). Only you can read or change your record. You can download everything or delete your account, together with all data stored with it, at any time on your account page (travelnow.info/account/).

    The mobile app

    The iOS and Android app shows the same maps and tools and works from the data stored on your phone. Two things are specific to the app, and neither is switched on until you agree to it:

    • Notifications. If you turn them on (Account page), we store a push token for your phone together with its platform (iOS or Android), your interface language and time zone, so a message reaches the right phone at a sensible hour. We use it only to tell you when a visa rule changes for your passport, a watched country or your planned trip, or when a government's safety advice changes for a country on your trip — never for marketing. Turning notifications off, signing out or deleting your account removes the token.
    • Ads. The app shows ads from Google AdMob on some screens. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier. On iOS the app first asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites (you can say no and lose nothing); in the EU and UK Google's consent form asks before any personalized ad is requested. You can change these choices in your phone's settings. How Google uses data in ads

    The app does not include the website's analytics scripts.

    Information third parties collect

    We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

    • Google AdSense serves the ads on this website (the app uses AdMob, described above). AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to personalize the ads you see and to measure performance. Google Privacy Policy · Manage your ad settings
    • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site. Cloudflare logs basic request data (IP, user-agent, referrer) for ~24 hours for security and performance. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
    • Stripe handles Pro-tier billing. We never see card data. Stripe sets its own cookies on the Checkout and customer portal pages. Stripe Privacy Policy
    • Resend delivers all transactional emails (confirmation, digests, unsubscribe receipts). They receive your address and the rendered message body. Resend Privacy Policy
    • Google Firebase handles sign-in (including the SMS code for phone sign-in), stores account data if you create an account, and delivers notifications to the app. Firebase privacy and security
    • Affiliate partners — when our service partner links (iVisa, Airalo, etc.) become available, clicking one takes you to the partner's site and they may set their own cookies. We may earn a commission on purchases you make there. These are clearly labeled "Sponsored" wherever they appear.

    Cookies

    travelnow.info sets first-party cookies through Google Analytics (`_ga`, `_ga_*` — measurement only, IP-anonymised) and Matomo (`_pk_*` — measurement only). Google AdSense and any affiliate partners may set cookies when you interact with their content. If you are visiting from the European Economy Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you will see a consent banner before any personalized cookies are loaded; you can decline and the site continues to work normally.

    Children's privacy

    travelnow.info is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

    Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

    Without an account we don't store personal data, so there is nothing for you to access, rectify, or delete on our side. With an account you can download or delete everything stored with it at any time on your account page. For data held by AdSense or our hosting provider, please contact them directly using the policy links above.

    Contact

    Questions about this policy: email hello@travelnow.info or open an issue on GitHub. See also our Terms of Use.

    Changes to this policy

    If we change anything material we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Substantial changes will be flagged on the site for at least 14 days before taking effect.
