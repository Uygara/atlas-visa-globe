    Privacy Policy

    Last updated 24 September 2026

    travelnow.info is a free visa and travel reference. This policy explains which personal data the website and the mobile app process, why, and how you can control it. Every tool works without an account, and without one we store no personal data about you.

    1. Who is responsible

    The controller is Uygar Atalay, who runs travelnow.info. For anything related to your data, write to hello@travelnow.info. We answer within 30 days.

    2. Using the site without an account

    • Your choices stay on your device. The passport you pick, the countries you watch, your planned trip and your settings are kept in your browser's local storage. We do not receive them.
    • Time zone. On a first visit the site reads your device's time zone to suggest a passport. This happens in your browser and nothing is sent to us.
    • Hosting. Cloudflare delivers and protects the site. To do so it processes technical request data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested.
    • Statistics. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, and with Google Analytics 4. In the EU and EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Turkey, Google Analytics runs without cookies and stores nothing on your device. Elsewhere it may set the first-party cookies _ga and _ga_*. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

    3. If you create an account

    An account keeps your saved items on all your devices. We store the email address or phone number you sign in with and the items you choose to save: passport and passport type, residence permits, watched countries, planned trip, Schengen calculator entries, language and theme. We never ask for passport numbers, documents or payment details.

    You can sign in with Google, with an email address and password, or with a phone number and a one-time code sent by SMS. Accounts run on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Saved items are stored in Google's European Union region, and access rules allow only you to read or change them.

    On the account page you can download your data or delete the account together with everything stored in it. Deletion takes effect immediately. We process this data to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

    4. Notifications

    Notifications stay off until you turn them on in your browser or in the app. When you do, we store a notification token for that device together with its platform (web, iOS or Android), your interface language and your time zone, so that a message reaches the right device at a reasonable hour. We notify you only when a visa rule changes for your passport or a country you watch, or when government safety advice changes for a country on your trip. We never send marketing. The token is removed when you turn notifications off, sign out or delete your account. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

    5. Advertising

    The website uses Google AdSense and the app uses Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google and its partners may use cookies or your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads, including ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. In the EU and EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads are shown only after you agree to them in Google's consent message. On iOS the app asks for your permission before any tracking, and saying no changes nothing else. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

    6. Service providers

    We rely on two providers, each bound by its own privacy terms and, where the law requires, by data processing terms:

    • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting, content delivery, security and web analytics.
    • Google (Google Ireland Ltd and Google LLC): Firebase for sign-in, account storage and notifications, Google Analytics, AdSense and AdMob.

    We do not sell personal data, and we share it with no one else unless the law requires it. Links to other websites, such as embassies, official application portals or our Buy Me a Coffee page, lead to services with their own privacy policies.

    7. International transfers

    Google and Cloudflare may process data in the United States and other countries. When data leaves the EU and EEA, the United Kingdom or Turkey, they rely on safeguards recognised by law, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

    8. How long we keep data

    • Account data: until you delete the account.
    • Notification tokens: until you turn notifications off, sign out or delete the account, or until the token stops working.
    • Google Analytics data: no longer than 14 months.
    • Emails you send us: as long as needed to reply and follow up.

    9. Your rights

    Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, receive a copy of it, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do yourself on the account page. For anything else, write to hello@travelnow.info. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Turkey this is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK). Residents of California have the right to know about and delete their personal information, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

    10. Children

    travelnow.info is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

    11. Security

    All connections use HTTPS, each account record can be read and changed only by its owner, and we collect only what the service needs. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    12. Changes

    We update this policy when the service or the law changes, and the date at the top shows the current version. If a change affects data you have already given us, we will point it out on the site and in the app.

    See also our Terms of Use.
