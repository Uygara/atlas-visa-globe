Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-21 · Operator: travelnow.info

What this site is

Atlas (travelnow.info) is a free reference tool that shows visa requirements for travelers worldwide. The dataset is rebuilt every 24 hours from authoritative public sources. We do not require an account, and we do not ask for personal information.

Information we collect ourselves

Almost nothing. We don't run our own analytics or trackers. With your permission, the browser's geolocation API may be used to pre-select a likely passport — the coordinates are sent only to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim reverse-geocode endpoint and immediately discarded by us. If you deny the prompt, we fall back to your browser's IANA time zone, used locally in your browser for the same purpose; that value is never sent to us.

If you subscribe to alerts

The Atlas alerts service stores only what's needed to deliver mail: your email address, the country codes you chose to watch, your tier (free or Pro), and timestamps. Pro subscribers' Stripe customer + subscription IDs are also stored so we can manage billing — Stripe holds the actual card data, never Atlas. Records live in Cloudflare KV in the EU region.

Every alert email contains a one-click unsubscribe link that deletes your record immediately and (if applicable) cancels your Stripe subscription.

Information third parties collect

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Google AdSense serves the ads on this site. AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to personalize the ads you see and to measure performance. Google Privacy Policy · Manage your ad settings
  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site. Cloudflare logs basic request data (IP, user-agent, referrer) for ~24 hours for security and performance. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Stripe handles Pro-tier billing. We never see card data. Stripe sets its own cookies on the Checkout and customer portal pages. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Resend delivers all transactional emails (confirmation, digests, unsubscribe receipts). They receive your address and the rendered message body. Resend Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim is only contacted when you explicitly grant the geolocation prompt. Your coordinates are sent there once, never stored on Atlas's side.
  • Affiliate partners — when our service partner links (iVisa, Airalo, etc.) become available, clicking one takes you to the partner's site and they may set their own cookies. We may earn a commission on purchases you make there. These are clearly labeled "Sponsored" wherever they appear.

Cookies

Atlas itself sets no first-party cookies. Google AdSense and any affiliate partners may set cookies when you interact with their content. If you are visiting from the European Economy Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you will see a consent banner before any personalized cookies are loaded; you can decline and the site continues to work normally.

Children's privacy

Atlas is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since we don't store personal data, there is nothing for you to access, rectify, or delete on our side. For data held by AdSense or our hosting provider, please contact them directly using the policy links above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at github.com/Uygara/atlas-visa-globe (a dedicated email address is being set up).

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Substantial changes will be flagged on the site for at least 14 days before taking effect.
