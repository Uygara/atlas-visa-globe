Digital nomad visas: 34 countries compared

Every active digital-nomad / remote-worker visa programme in 2026, with the numbers that actually matter: monthly income floor, application fee, first-grant duration, tax break, and whether you can bring family.

Reviewed on 22 May 2026; Portugal, Spain, Croatia and Malta re-checked in November 2026. Sources are linked in each row.
Country ↕ Programme Min income / mo Fee Duration Tax Family travelnow.info link

How to read the table. Income is the minimum monthly proof of earnings, converted to USD at publication; amounts marked € are the official euro figure, not converted. Fees are application fees only. Most programmes also require health insurance ($30–100/month typical) and, in some cases, an apartment lease. "Family OK" means a spouse + dependent children can be added under the primary applicant.

Tax-break summaries are deliberately short. Every programme has fine print, double-tax treaties, and residency-day thresholds that change the real outcome. Always run the numbers with a local accountant before deciding.

Spotted an outdated figure or a missing programme? Open an issue at github.com/Uygara/atlas-visa-globe.
