    Visa-change alerts

    Get a notification when a visa rule changes for your passport or for a country you watch, and when government safety advice changes for a country on your trip. Alerts are free and need no email address.

    • Sent only when something changes, at most once a day per device.
    • Never during the night on your device's clock, and never marketing.
    • Rules are checked every 24 hours against public sources.
    • You can turn them off at any time, here or in your browser settings.
