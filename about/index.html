About Atlas

Free · Open data · Daily refresh

Atlas is an interactive visa-requirements explorer. Pick your passport and see at a glance which of the world's 200+ countries you can enter visa-free, which need an eVisa, which issue a visa on arrival, and which require a full embassy application. We do this for every passport — not just the major ones.

How the data is built

Atlas aggregates visa-policy information from authoritative, regularly-updated public sources covering every recognised passport. The full dataset is rebuilt every 24 hours so the map you see is never more than a day behind the latest announcements. The "Recently changed" feed in the side panel surfaces the previous day's diff.

If something on a country page looks off, get in touch — we'd like to hear about it and fix it quickly.

What Atlas is not

  • Not an official source. Visa policies change frequently and unevenly. Always confirm with the destination's embassy or consulate before booking.
  • Not a visa application service. We don't apply on your behalf. When a destination needs an eVisa or VR, we may link to a trusted partner (iVisa, VisaHQ) who handles applications — those are clearly marked "Sponsored".

Why it's free

Server costs are negligible (Cloudflare Pages free tier). We cover them through unobtrusive ads served by Google AdSense and occasional affiliate commissions when readers sign up for travel services we'd recommend anyway (eSIMs, insurance, visa-application help).
