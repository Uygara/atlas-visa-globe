    About travelnow.info

    Free · Open data · Daily refresh

    travelnow.info is an interactive visa-requirements explorer. Pick your passport and see at a glance which of the world's 200+ countries you can enter visa-free, which need an eVisa, which issue a visa on arrival, and which require a full embassy application. We do this for every passport — not just the major ones.

    Who's behind this

    travelnow.info is an independent project built and maintained by Uygar Atalay. It started from a simple frustration: every time I planned a trip, working out who needs a visa for where meant digging through scattered government pages and out-of-date forum posts. I wanted one honest map that just answers the question — so I built it, and I keep it running. It's a one-person project, not a company, and I read every message that comes in.

    How the data is built

    travelnow.info aggregates visa-policy information from authoritative, regularly-updated public sources covering every recognised passport. A scheduled job rebuilds the full dataset every 24 hours, so the map you see is never more than a day behind the latest changes we have recorded. On top of the automated data we maintain a hand-curated correction layer for the cases machines get wrong — territories, freedom-of-movement zones, residence-permit upgrades, and entry bans — each one checked against an official source before it ships. The "Recently changed" feed in the side panel surfaces the previous day's differences.

    We have one hard rule: we never invent visa data. If a fact can't be sourced, we leave it out rather than guess. If something on a country page looks wrong, email me with the passport, the destination and ideally a source link — corrections get priority and usually ship the same week.

    What travelnow.info is not

    • Not an official source. Visa policies change frequently and unevenly. Always confirm with the destination's embassy or consulate before booking.
    • Not a visa application service. We don't apply on your behalf. When a destination needs an eVisa or VR, we may link to a trusted partner (iVisa, VisaHQ) who handles applications — those are clearly marked "Sponsored".

    Why it's free

    Server costs are negligible (Cloudflare Pages free tier). We cover them through unobtrusive ads served by Google AdSense and occasional affiliate commissions when readers sign up for travel services we'd recommend anyway (eSIMs, insurance, visa-application help).

    If the site saved you time or a wrong booking, you can buy me a coffee — completely optional and the site stays free either way.
