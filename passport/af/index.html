A Afghanistan passport currently gives its holder access to about 12 of the 205 destinations we track without arranging a visa beforehand: 4 visa-free and 8 with a visa on arrival. Beyond those, 58 destinations offer an eVisa you apply for online (counting them, that is 70 destinations with no embassy visit), and 135 still require a traditional embassy visa. By visa-free access it ranks #199 of 200 passports worldwide.

Looking at it region by region, the Afghanistan passport is strongest in Oceania, where 4 of 16 countries are open without a prior visa, and most limited in South America (0 of 13). The full regional picture: Oceania (4/16), the Americas (North & Central) (2/25), Africa (4/55), Asia (2/49), Europe (0/47), South America (0/13).

Among the destinations travellers ask about most, you can travel without a visa (or get one on arrival) to Egypt.

You should plan ahead for a full visa to United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, and Spain and an eVisa for India, Thailand, Malaysia, and South Africa. Sort these out before you book non-refundable travel.

If a destination shows eVisa or visa on arrival below, you usually don't need to visit an embassy: an eVisa is applied for on the destination's official portal and arrives by email, while a visa on arrival is issued at the airport or land border (carry the fee and a passport valid for at least six months). Our Visa Shortcuts tool also shows when a visa or residence permit you already hold can unlock easier entry elsewhere.

Afghanistan passport: frequently asked questions

How many countries can Afghanistan passport holders visit without a visa?

Around 12 of the 205 destinations we track need no visa arranged in advance: 4 visa-free and 8 with a visa on arrival. A further 58 offer an eVisa online.

Do Afghanistan passport holders need a visa for the United States?

Travellers on a Afghanistan passport need a visa for the United States arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate. The US visa-waiver programme also requires an approved ESTA travel authorization even when no visa is needed: our ESTA checker covers who qualifies.

Can you travel to Europe (the Schengen Area) on a Afghanistan passport?

Germany stands in for the Schengen Area: holders of a Afghanistan passport need a visa for Germany arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate. From 2026 the EU's ETIAS travel authorization also applies to many visa-free visitors: see our ETIAS checker.

How is this ranked, and how current is the data?

Passports are ranked by how many destinations they can enter without applying for a visa: visa-free, with a national ID card, with an online travel authorization or with a visa on arrival (the same measure passport indexes use); ties are broken by eVisa access. It is the same number and the same ranking the interactive map shows. Figures are rebuilt every 24 hours from public visa-policy sources, so this page reflects the most recent change we have recorded. Always confirm with the destination's embassy before booking.
