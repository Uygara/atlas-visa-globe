A Montenegro passport currently gives its holder access to about 96 of the 205 destinations we track without arranging a visa beforehand: 79 visa-free, 5 with just a national ID card, and 12 with a visa on arrival. Beyond those, 69 destinations offer an eVisa you apply for online (counting them, that is 165 destinations with no embassy visit), and 40 still require a traditional embassy visa. By visa-free access it ranks #84 of 200 passports worldwide.

Looking at it region by region, the Montenegro passport is strongest in Europe, where 43 of 46 countries are open without a prior visa, and most limited in Africa (7 of 55). The full regional picture: Europe (43/46), South America (8/13), the Americas (North & Central) (13/25), Asia (19/50), Oceania (6/16), Africa (7/55).

Among the destinations travellers ask about most, you can travel without a visa (or get one on arrival) to Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Switzerland, Japan, and China.

You should plan ahead for a full visa to United States, United Kingdom, Canada, New Zealand, Indonesia, and Mexico and an eVisa for Australia, South Korea, India, Saudi Arabia, and Thailand. Sort these out before you book non-refundable travel.

If a destination shows eVisa or visa on arrival below, you usually don't need to visit an embassy: an eVisa is applied for on the destination's official portal and arrives by email, while a visa on arrival is issued at the airport or land border (carry the fee and a passport valid for at least six months). Our Visa Shortcuts tool also shows when a visa or residence permit you already hold can unlock easier entry elsewhere.

Montenegro passport: frequently asked questions

How many countries can Montenegro passport holders visit without a visa?

Around 96 of the 205 destinations we track need no visa arranged in advance: 79 visa-free, 5 with just a national ID card, and 12 with a visa on arrival. A further 69 offer an eVisa online.

Do Montenegro passport holders need a visa for the United States?

Travellers on a Montenegro passport need a visa for the United States arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate. The US visa-waiver programme also requires an approved ESTA travel authorization even when no visa is needed: our ESTA checker covers who qualifies.

Can you travel to Europe (the Schengen Area) on a Montenegro passport?

Germany stands in for the Schengen Area: holders of a Montenegro passport can enter Germany visa-free for up to 90 days. From 2026 the EU's ETIAS travel authorization also applies to many visa-free visitors: see our ETIAS checker.

How is this ranked, and how current is the data?

Passports are ranked by how many destinations they can enter without applying for a visa: visa-free, with a national ID card, with an online travel authorization or with a visa on arrival (the same measure passport indexes use); ties are broken by eVisa access. It is the same number and the same ranking the interactive map shows. Figures are rebuilt every 24 hours from public visa-policy sources, so this page reflects the most recent change we have recorded. Always confirm with the destination's embassy before booking.
