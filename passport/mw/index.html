    A Malawi passport currently gives its holder access to about 50 of the 205 destinations we track without arranging a visa beforehand — 39 visa-free and 11 with a visa on arrival. Beyond those, 54 destinations offer an eVisa you apply for online, and 101 still require a traditional embassy visa. With 104 destinations open without an embassy visa, it ranks #131 of 200 passports worldwide.

    Looking at it region by region, the Malawi passport is strongest in the Americas (North & Central), where 12 of 25 countries are open without a prior visa, and most limited in Europe (2 of 47). The full regional picture: the Americas (North & Central) (12/25), Africa (22/54), Oceania (5/16), Asia (8/50), South America (1/13), Europe (2/47).

    Among the destinations travellers ask about most, you can travel without a visa (or get one on arrival) to Singapore, Malaysia, and South Africa.

    You should plan ahead for a full visa to United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, and Spain and an eVisa for India, United Arab Emirates, and Thailand. Sort these out before you book non-refundable travel.

    If a destination shows eVisa or visa on arrival below, you usually don't need to visit an embassy: an eVisa is applied for on the destination's official portal and arrives by email, while a visa on arrival is issued at the airport or land border (carry the fee and a passport valid for at least six months). Our Visa Shortcuts tool also shows when a visa or residence permit you already hold can unlock easier entry elsewhere.

    Malawi passport — frequently asked questions

    How many countries can Malawi passport holders visit without a visa?

    Around 50 of the 205 destinations we track need no visa arranged in advance — 39 visa-free and 11 with a visa on arrival. A further 54 offer an eVisa online.

    Do Malawi passport holders need a visa for the United States?

    Travellers on a Malawi passport need a visa arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate for the United States. The US visa-waiver programme also requires an approved ESTA travel authorization even when no visa is needed — our ESTA checker covers who qualifies.

    Can you travel to Europe (the Schengen Area) on a Malawi passport?

    For Germany, a representative Schengen country, holders of a Malawi passport need a visa arranged in advance at an embassy or consulate. From 2026 the EU's ETIAS travel authorization also applies to many visa-free visitors — see our ETIAS checker.

    How is this ranked, and how current is the data?

    Passports are ranked by how many destinations they can enter without an embassy visa — visa-free, with a national ID card, with an online travel authorization, with an eVisa or with a visa on arrival — and ties are broken by how many of those need nothing arranged in advance. It is the same ranking the interactive map shows. Figures are rebuilt every 24 hours from public visa-policy sources, so this page reflects the most recent change we have recorded. Always confirm with the destination's embassy before booking.
