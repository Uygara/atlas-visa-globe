Schengen visa appointment alert

In Türkiye, a Schengen visa appointment can take weeks to find, and free slots are often gone within minutes. We are considering a paid alert that tells you when appointments open at the visa centre you need.

This service does not exist yet. We will only build it if enough people want it. Join the list and we will email you once, when it opens. If we decide not to build it, we delete the list.

How it would work

  • You choose the country you are applying to and the city of your visa centre.
  • When appointments open there, you get an email and, if you use our app, a notification.
  • We would only use sources the visa centres allow. We would never ask for your visa-centre password and would not book an appointment for you.
  • Planned price: around 199 TL a month, cancel any time. Tell us below what you would pay.
